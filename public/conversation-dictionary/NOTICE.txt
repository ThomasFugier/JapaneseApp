Japanese conversation dictionary data

The lookup entries in v1 are derived from JMdict, maintained by the
Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG), and distributed
under the JMdict/EDRDG license:
https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html

Source distribution: scriptin/jmdict-simplified release
3.6.2+20261109214828, published 2026-11-09:
https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified/releases/tag/3.6.2%2B20261109214828

The source project states that JMdict-derived files retain the original
JMdict license. Its conversion code and other project files are CC BY-SA 4.0;
that license does not replace the JMdict data license:
https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified/blob/master/README.md#license

The build-time prepared data retains Japanese headwords/readings, parts of
speech, and French or English glosses. Where an entry has French glosses,
those are used; common English entries fill gaps. Full English data is used
only for entries tagged as particles, auxiliaries, or copulas, to improve
function-word coverage. The compact output caps headwords/readings and
senses/glosses per entry. It is a vocabulary lookup resource, not a sentence
translation. Source archives and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in the generated
v1/manifest.json.
